STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: SOP-042
TITLE: Industrial Unit Thermal Overheating Response Protocol
DOCUMENT ID: SOP-042-REV-3
APPLICABILITY: All Machine 101 Units and High-Capacity Industrial Cooling Modules

1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
This standard operating procedure defines mandatory engineering protocols when Machine 101 exhibits thermal variance or exceeds standard operating boundaries.

2. TEMPERATURE BOUNDARIES
- Optimal Band: 60°C to 75°C
- Nominal Upper Bound: 80°C
- Thermal Warning Threshold: > 80°C (Requires Level-1 Operator Intervention)
- Critical Safety Trip Threshold: > 95°C (Requires Immediate Emergency Stop)

3. STEP-BY-STEP OPERATIONAL RESPONSE
Phase 1: Warning Band Detection (80°C - 95°C)
1. Verify Cooling Fan operation: Ensure fan RPM is between 1200 and 1400 RPM.
2. Inspect intake louvers and radiator grill for dust, lint, or debris obstruction.
3. Check coolant reservoir fluid level: Must remain between 60% and 100% capacity.
4. Verify temperature sensor thermocouple calibration using an infrared handheld probe.

Phase 2: Critical Trip Condition (> 95°C)
1. Press emergency stop circuit immediately.
2. Isolate main power breaker Q101.
3. Initiate incident logging in the local compliance portal.
4. Do not restart without written authorization from the Chief Maintenance Engineer.

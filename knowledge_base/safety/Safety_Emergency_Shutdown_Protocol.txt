PLANT SAFETY DIRECTIVE: PSD-88
TITLE: Machine 101 Emergency Power & Thermal Hazard Isolation
CLASSIFICATION: Confidential Internal Safety Standard

1. HAZARD IDENTIFICATION
Thermal runaway in Machine 101 poses fire and component degradation risks if coolant pressure drops below 1.5 bar or temperatures sustain above 95°C for longer than 60 seconds.

2. EMERGENCY SHUTDOWN PROCEDURE
1. Trip the Master Emergency Stop (E-STOP) button on the main control panel.
2. Confirm cooling fan housing is clear before opening casing.
3. Check coolant lines for visible rupture, fluid misting, or joint leaks.
4. Notify plant safety officer and log timestamped telemetry readings.

3. RE-COMMISSIONING CHECKLIST
- Coolant pressure verified between 2.0 bar and 3.5 bar.
- Vibration levels confirmed below 0.30 mm/s.
- Radiator cleaned with dry compressed air.
- Sign-off on Maintenance Log Form ML-101.

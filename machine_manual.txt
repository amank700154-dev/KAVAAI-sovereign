MACHINE 101 - MAINTENANCE MANUAL

Machine: Industrial Cooling Unit 101

Normal operating temperature:
60°C to 80°C

Warning temperature:
Above 80°C

Critical temperature:
Above 95°C

Maintenance schedule:
The cooling system should be inspected every 30 days.

If temperature exceeds 80°C:
1. Check the cooling fan.
2. Check for dust or blockage.
3. Check coolant level.
4. Inspect the temperature sensor.

If temperature exceeds 95°C:
Immediately stop the machine and perform a safety inspection.

Common causes of overheating:
- Cooling fan failure
- Dust accumulation
- Low coolant level
- Faulty temperature sensor
